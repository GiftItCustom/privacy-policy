Privacy Policy

GiftItCustom Growth Agent — Last updated: June 11, 2026

GiftItCustom Growth Agent ("we", "our", "the app") is a tool that automatically creates and publishes pins to Pinterest based on product listings from the GiftItCustom shop on Etsy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how you can control or remove that access.

1. Information We Collect

Our app accesses data from two sources:

a) From Pinterest (via Pinterest's official OAuth process), we may access:

  • Your Pinterest profile information (username, account ID, profile picture)
  • Your Pinterest boards (names and IDs, for the purpose of selecting where to publish)
  • Pins created or managed through our app (titles, descriptions, images, links)
  • Basic analytics related to pins published by our app (impressions, clicks, saves), where permitted by the scopes you approve

b) From Etsy (via the GiftItCustom shop's product listings), we access:

  • Publicly available product information: titles, descriptions, images, prices, and listing URLs
  • Product category and tag information used to select relevant Pinterest boards

No Etsy customer data, order information, or buyer personal data is accessed, stored, or processed by this app — only publicly listed shop/product information used to generate pins.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data above is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your Pinterest account via Pinterest's OAuth flow
  • Automatically generate pins (image, title, description, and link back to the corresponding Etsy listing) for new or existing GiftItCustom products
  • Publish these pins to the Pinterest boards you select
  • Display basic performance metrics for the pins published through our app

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core function described above.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • OAuth access tokens (Pinterest) are stored securely and encrypted at rest.
  • Product data pulled from Etsy is used to generate pin content and may be temporarily cached to avoid duplicate posting; it is not shared or sold.
  • Tokens and associated account data are retained only for as long as your account remains connected to our app.
  • We apply industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except:

  • Where required to operate the core service (e.g. cloud hosting and infrastructure providers, who are bound by confidentiality obligations)
  • As necessary to interact with Pinterest's and Etsy's APIs to perform the functions described above
  • Where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may revoke our app's access to your Pinterest account at any time via Pinterest Account Settings → Apps. Once access is revoked, we will delete stored Pinterest access tokens and associated profile data within 30 days. Cached product data from Etsy is retained only as long as needed for pin generation and deduplication, and is deleted on the same schedule. To request earlier deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent (by disconnecting the app from Pinterest)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at individuals under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from such individuals.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
